Multiband Impedance Matching Networks for RF Energy Harvesting

Challenge

To reduce the dependency on batteries for implants. Invisible waves from radios, TVs, cellular towers, and Wi-Fi constantly surround us. Is it possible to capture this energy and power miniature devices?

Solution

A matching network that can function on different power and frequency ranges to extract maximum power from an RF energy source. Various impedance-matching networks are available for specific power and frequency applications; however, the most exciting aspect is creating a matching network that can function on several frequencies and numerous power levels.

My contribution

I am currently working on implementing a multiband impedance matching network to achieve maximum power transfer under varying conditions. This technology will create a future with miniature battery-free electronics.
